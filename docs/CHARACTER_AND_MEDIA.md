# Service Excellence Guide — artwork and media approval

The source is the original front-cover illustration (PDF page 1): a smiling Jamaican Black woman with braided hair, a headset and boom microphone, a pale grey-blue tailored blazer, white shirt, dark trousers and open hands.

The vector at assets/guide.svg is a **provisional animated concept, not the exact extracted cover artwork**. It preserves the overall presentation while the artwork rights and layered high-resolution reference are confirmed. Final production must reuse the approved original likeness and preserve facial features, braids, complexion, headset and clothing.

The current welcome narration uses the browser's text-to-speech facility plus a visible transcript and playback controls. This is not a lip-synchronised production video or the final approved Jamaican voice.

## Draft welcoming script

“Hello, and welcome to Service Excellence in Action. At the Ministry of Health and Wellness, good service begins with how we support one another. Our 2025 Internal Customer Satisfaction Study reports an overall satisfaction score of 54 percent. Let's explore the five service dimensions, consider what staff shared, and review the recommendations for improving internal services. The findings are our starting point; the recommendations are proposed next steps, not completed achievements. Together, we can turn research into meaningful action.”

This script is creative editorial text, not a quotation from the research report. Obtain approval for the final name, appearance, voice and full video collection before production.

## Final multimedia acceptance criteria

- Exact Ministry-approved character design.
- Appropriately licensed and approved Jamaican professional voice.
- Natural lip sync, blink, head movement and controlled hand gestures.
- 1080p source, compressed web variants and poster image.
- User-controlled playback and captions (VTT), mute, volume, restart and transcript.
- No forced autoplay; works with keyboard, screen readers and reduced motion.
- Responsive and low-bandwidth fallback on mobile.
