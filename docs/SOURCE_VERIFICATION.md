# Research source verification and publication governance

Authority: Ministry of Health and Wellness, *Internal Customer Satisfaction Study Report* (2025 study, 2026 cover, 74 PDF pages). The application's single source-of-truth is src/data.js.

## Verified figures
- Survey sampling frame: 766 employees; selected sample: 332 (119 supervisors, 213 non-supervisors); respondents: 229. Printed p. 6 / PDF p. 19.
- Survey interactions: 219 respondents reported interaction with services, resulting in 1,133 recorded service ratings/interactions. Printed p. 11 / PDF p. 24.
- Satisfied ratings: 611 of 1,133; reported CSAT 54%. Printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 26–27.
- Service dimensions, in report order: Communication 59%, Access and Facilities 55%, Quality and Reliability 59%, Timeliness/Responsiveness 48%, Cost and Payment Processes 44%. Figure 8 / Table 3.
- Divisional CSAT: Executive Management 71%, EEHCSD 60%, NPHL 60%, Standards and Regulation 60%, Technical Services 60%, Policy Planning and Development 59%, Corporate Services 46%, Finance and Accounts 28%. Table 2 / PDF p. 27.
- Ten focus groups had 63 participants. Ten numbered implementation workstreams appear in Appendix 5, printed pp. 57–61 / PDF pp. 70–74.

## Issues requiring research-team clarification
1. Survey response rate: the report says 68%; 229/332 = 68.98% and rounds to 69%. Retain reported wording with a note pending confirmation.
2. Focus-group sex composition: narrative lists 38 female + 15 male = 53; Figure 10 shows 46 female + 17 male = 63. The prototype omits the distribution.
3. Figure 5 shows Corporate Services 22% and Technical Services 34%, whereas Table 2 gives 400/1,133 (35%) and 237/1,133 (21%). Do not equate these or present contested visual comparisons.
4. Distinguish 229 survey participants, 219 reporting interactions, and 1,133 service ratings. Some narrative labels use 'respondents' for ratings.
5. Nine recommendation headings in the main text versus ten numbered Table 8 workstreams. The app uses Table 8.
6. Discussion page 41 mentions 35% for Cost and Payment Processes; Figure 8/Table 3 show 44%. The prototype uses the consistent chart/table figure and flags the difference.

## Mandatory approvals before public release
- Confirm public or staff-only access classification, specifically for the complete PDF, focus-group quotations and detailed divisional data.
- Ministry identity, naming, original source acknowledgement and co-branding.
- Research-team adjudication of discrepancies.
- Exact character artwork, final name, voice, script and captions.
- Hosting provider, external QR service, privacy notice, accessibility testing, data/feedback handling, and institutional security review.
- Any future implementation-tracking features must receive actual authorised progress data.

The public repository stores an aggregate-only technology preview, not the full internal report, recorded participant feedback or original survey records. No achievements or live progress claims are made.
